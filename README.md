Hi there! I'm Dahye Kim, the creator of this webpage. 
This project blends the concept of **"Your Tattoo Inspiration"** with a birth flower gallery.
In tattoo shops, many clients look to incorporate meaningful elements like the names of family members or partners,
alongside significant dates like birthdays or anniversaries.
Birth flowers frequently play a key role in these designs. 

Inspired by this, I designed a page where users can browse beautiful flower photography for inspiration
while actively experimenting with and choosing different tattoo-friendly fonts.
Thank you for visiting!
